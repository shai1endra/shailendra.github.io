Duskwick Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026. Publisher: Rateshive. Contact: info@rateshive.com

Duskwick is a single-player game. It has no accounts, and we do not collect, store or sell personal data on our servers. Your game progress is saved only on your device.

Advertising

Duskwick shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and device and usage information to serve and measure ads. In regions where required (EEA, UK), we show Google's consent form first, and you can choose non-personalised ads. You can reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads). See Google's privacy policy.

Children

Duskwick is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes on this page. Questions: info@rateshive.com
